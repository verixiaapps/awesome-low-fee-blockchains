## Zero/Near-Zero Fees

These blockchains offer truly feeless or near-zero fee transactions.

### Nano (XNO)
- **Average Fee:** $0.00 (feeless)
- **Consensus:** Open Representative Voting (ORV)
- **Finality:** ~1 second
- **Website:** [nano.org](https://nano.org)
- **Use Case:** Peer-to-peer payments, micropayments
- **Note:** Energy-efficient, no mining. Fee-free by design.

### IOTA
- **Average Fee:** $0.00 (feeless)
- **Consensus:** Tangle (DAG-based)
- **Finality:** ~10 seconds
- **Website:** [iota.org](https://www.iota.org)
- **Use Case:** IoT, machine-to-machine payments
- **Note:** Designed for IoT microtransactions. Fee-free architecture.

### EOS
- **Average Fee:** Near $0.00
- **Consensus:** Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS)
- **Finality:** ~0.5 seconds
- **Website:** [eos.io](https://eos.io)
- **Use Case:** DApps, gaming
- **Note:** Users stake tokens for network resources instead of paying fees.

### TRON (TRX)
- **Average Fee:** Near $0.00
- **Consensus:** Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS)
- **Finality:** ~3 seconds
- **Website:** [tron.network](https://tron.network)
- **Use Case:** DApps, content, USDT transfers
- **Note:** Bandwidth/energy system. Free daily bandwidth allocation.

## Sub-Cent Fees ($0.001 - $0.01)

Extremely low fees, suitable for frequent transactions.

### Stellar (XLM)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.000004
- **Consensus:** Stellar Consensus Protocol (SCP)
- **Finality:** 3-5 seconds
- **Website:** [stellar.org](https://stellar.org)
- **Use Case:** Cross-border payments, tokenization
- **Note:** Built for remittances. Fixed fee of 0.00001 XLM.

### Ripple (XRP)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.00003
- **Consensus:** Ripple Protocol Consensus Algorithm
- **Finality:** 3-5 seconds
- **Website:** [ripple.com](https://ripple.com)
- **Use Case:** Bank transfers, institutional payments
- **Note:** Designed for financial institutions. Extremely low fees.

### Harmony (ONE)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.0001
- **Consensus:** Effective Proof of Stake
- **Finality:** 2 seconds
- **Website:** [harmony.one](https://harmony.one)
- **Use Case:** DApps, DeFi, NFTs
- **Note:** Sharded architecture for scalability.

### Algorand (ALGO)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.0002
- **Consensus:** Pure Proof of Stake
- **Finality:** ~3.9 seconds
- **Website:** [algorand.com](https://www.algorand.com)
- **Use Case:** DeFi, NFTs, CBDCs
- **Note:** Carbon-negative blockchain. Consistent low fees.

### Solana (SOL)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.00025
- **Consensus:** Proof of History + Proof of Stake
- **Finality:** ~0.4 seconds
- **Website:** [solana.com](https://solana.com)
- **Use Case:** DeFi, NFTs, high-frequency trading
- **Note:** Extremely high throughput (65,000+ TPS). Fees remain low even during congestion.

### NEAR Protocol
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.001
- **Consensus:** Nightshade (sharded PoS)
- **Finality:** 1-2 seconds
- **Website:** [near.org](https://near.org)
- **Use Case:** DApps, DeFi, AI agents
- **Note:** Human-readable accounts. Developer-friendly.

### Hedera (HBAR)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.001
- **Consensus:** Hashgraph (aBFT)
- **Finality:** 3-5 seconds
- **Website:** [hedera.com](https://hedera.com)
- **Use Case:** Enterprise, tokenization, supply chain
- **Note:** Governed by global enterprises. Fixed, predictable fees.

### Polygon (MATIC)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.002
- **Consensus:** PoS with Heimdall & Bor
- **Finality:** ~2 seconds
- **Website:** [polygon.technology](https://polygon.technology)
- **Use Case:** Ethereum scaling, DeFi, NFTs
- **Note:** Ethereum sidechain. EVM-compatible with dramatically lower fees.

### TON (Toncoin)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.005
- **Consensus:** Byzantine Fault Tolerant
- **Finality:** ~5 seconds
- **Website:** [ton.org](https://ton.org)
- **Use Case:** Telegram integration, payments
- **Note:** Originally developed by Telegram. Growing ecosystem.

## Low Fees ($0.01 - $0.10)

Lower than Ethereum mainnet but higher than the cheapest options.

### Tezos (XTZ)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.01
- **Consensus:** Liquid Proof of Stake
- **Finality:** ~30 seconds
- **Website:** [tezos.com](https://tezos.com)
- **Use Case:** NFTs, DeFi, enterprise
- **Note:** Self-amending blockchain. No hard forks needed for upgrades.

### Avalanche (AVAX)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.02
- **Consensus:** Avalanche Consensus
- **Finality:** <1 second
- **Website:** [avax.network](https://www.avax.network)
- **Use Case:** DeFi, enterprise, subnets
- **Note:** Sub-second finality. Subnet architecture for customization.

### Fantom (FTM)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.02
- **Consensus:** Lachesis (aBFT)
- **Finality:** 1-2 seconds
- **Website:** [fantom.foundation](https://fantom.foundation)
- **Use Case:** DeFi, DApps
- **Note:** EVM-compatible. Fast and cheap.

### BNB Chain (BNB)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.05
- **Consensus:** Proof of Staked Authority
- **Finality:** ~3 seconds
- **Website:** [bnbchain.org](https://www.bnbchain.org)
- **Use Case:** DeFi, trading, gaming
- **Note:** High adoption. Binance ecosystem integration.

### Cardano (ADA)
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.15
- **Consensus:** Ouroboros (PoS)
- **Finality:** ~20 seconds
- **Website:** [cardano.org](https://cardano.org)
- **Use Case:** DeFi, identity, governance
- **Note:** Research-driven development. Deterministic fees.

## Layer 2 Solutions

Ethereum scaling solutions with significantly lower fees than L1.

### Arbitrum
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.05 - $0.30
- **Type:** Optimistic Rollup
- **Website:** [arbitrum.io](https://arbitrum.io)
- **Note:** Largest Ethereum L2 by TVL. Full EVM compatibility.

### Optimism
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.05 - $0.30
- **Type:** Optimistic Rollup
- **Website:** [optimism.io](https://www.optimism.io)
- **Note:** Part of the OP Stack/Superchain. Growing ecosystem.

### zkSync Era
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.10 - $0.30
- **Type:** ZK Rollup
- **Website:** [zksync.io](https://zksync.io)
- **Note:** Native account abstraction. ZK-proof security.

### Base
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.05 - $0.20
- **Type:** Optimistic Rollup (OP Stack)
- **Website:** [base.org](https://base.org)
- **Note:** Built by Coinbase. Growing rapidly.

### Polygon zkEVM
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.05 - $0.20
- **Type:** ZK Rollup
- **Website:** [polygon.technology/polygon-zkevm](https://polygon.technology/polygon-zkevm)
- **Note:** EVM-equivalent with ZK security.

### Linea
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.10 - $0.30
- **Type:** ZK Rollup
- **Website:** [linea.build](https://linea.build)
- **Note:** Built by Consensys. EVM-compatible.

### Scroll
- **Average Fee:** ~$0.05 - $0.25
- **Type:** ZK Rollup
- **Website:** [scroll.io](https://scroll.io)
- **Note:** Community-driven zkEVM.

### SKALE
- **Average Fee:** $0.00 (gas-free)
- **Type:** Elastic Sidechain
- **Website:** [skale.space](https://skale.space)
- **Note:** Offers gas-free transactions for end users.

## Choosing the Right Chain

### For Micropayments
- **Best:** Nano, IOTA, Stellar
- **Why:** Zero or near-zero fees make micro-transactions viable

### For DeFi
- **Best:** Solana, Polygon, Arbitrum
- **Why:** Low fees + EVM compatibility (Polygon/Arbitrum) or strong DeFi ecosystem (Solana)

### For NFTs
- **Best:** Polygon, Solana, Tezos
- **Why:** Low minting costs, established marketplaces

### For Gaming
- **Best:** Polygon, SKALE, Solana
- **Why:** Gas-free options (SKALE) or very low fees with high TPS

### For Enterprise
- **Best:** Hedera, Algorand, Avalanche
- **Why:** Governance, compliance features, predictable costs

### For Ethereum Developers
- **Best:** Arbitrum, Base, Polygon
- **Why:** Full EVM compatibility, existing tooling works
